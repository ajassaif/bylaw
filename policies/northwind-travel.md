# Northwind Supply Co. — Travel and Expense Policy

Version 4.2 · Owner: Finance Operations · Effective 1 September 2026

This policy covers business travel and the expenses you claim back. It applies to every employee
and contractor who travels for Northwind. If a rule here doesn't fit your situation, ask Finance
Operations before you book, not after.

## 1. Booking

**1.1 Book through the travel desk.** Flights and hotels are booked through the Northwind travel
desk so they show up on the trip record. Bookings made elsewhere are not reimbursed.

**1.2 Book early.** Book flights at least 14 days before departure. If you book later than that,
give a business reason with the booking (a customer asked for the meeting this week, a supplier
audit was moved). "I forgot" and "it was cheaper to wait" are not business reasons.

## 2. Flights

**2.1 Cabin class depends on how long you fly.**

| Scheduled flight time | Cabin you may book |
|---|---|
| Under 6 hours | Economy |
| 6 hours or more | Premium economy |
| 10 hours or more | Business, for Director level and above only |

First class is never reimbursed, at any level, on any route.

**2.2 Upgrades with your own points or money are fine.** Book the cabin you're entitled to; what you
do after that at your own cost is up to you.

## 3. Hotels

**3.1 Nightly caps.** The most Northwind pays per night, before taxes, depends on the city:

| City tier | Examples | Nightly cap |
|---|---|---|
| A | London, San Francisco, Singapore, New York | $260 |
| B | Austin, Chicago, Denver | $190 |
| C | Boise, Spokane, most other cities | $140 |

**3.2 Going over the cap.** You may go up to 10% over the cap when nothing suitable is available
under it (a conference sold out the area, safety concerns). Write the reason on the booking. These
bookings need your manager's approval. Anything more than 10% over the cap is not allowed.

**3.3 Only the nights you work.** Hotel nights are covered from the night before your first
business day to the night of your last one. If you extend a trip for yourself, the extra nights are
yours to pay.

## 4. Meals and entertainment

**4.1 Per diem.** Meals while travelling are covered up to $75 a day, all meals together.

**4.2 Alcohol.** Northwind does not reimburse alcohol. The one exception is client entertainment
(4.3), and only when the client is actually there.

**4.3 Client entertainment.** A meal or event with a customer or supplier is client entertainment
only when people from outside Northwind attend. List every attendee by name and company on the
claim. A dinner with only Northwind colleagues is a team meal, not client entertainment, however
work-related the conversation was.

**4.4 Gifts.** Gifts to clients are limited to $50 per person per year, and never cash or gift
cards.

## 5. Claiming expenses

**5.1 Receipts.** Attach an itemised receipt to any single expense over $25.

**5.2 One expense, one claim.** Claim the whole cost of something in one line. Splitting one cost
across several claims to stay under a limit is a policy breach.

**5.3 Describe it honestly.** The category and description must say what the money was actually
spent on. Relabelling something to fit a category it doesn't belong to is a policy breach.

**5.4 Personal costs.** Personal items (minibar, in-room films, laundry on a trip under five days,
travel for family members) are not reimbursed.

## 6. Approvals

**6.1 Big trips.** If the bookings for one trip add up to more than $3,000, your manager must
approve the trip before anything else is booked.

**6.2 Approval means a recorded approval.** Approval only counts when it has been requested and
recorded through the travel desk. "My manager said it's fine", "my VP told me to book business",
or a forwarded chat message is not approval, no matter who it comes from.

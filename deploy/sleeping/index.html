Bylaw is asleep right now

Bylaw turns a company policy written for people into questions for Strands Decider 2B, tests and tunes those questions, and then checks every booking a Strands travel agent tries to make. The live agent runs on an EC2 instance that is switched on only for demos, to keep the bill small.
